About the data

Each block group is colored by the number of jobs its residents can reach within 30 minutes by transit or by car, or by how many times more jobs a car reaches.

Transit

Door-to-door trips by walking and transit, including the walk, the wait, the ride and any transfer. The time is the median over departures between 7:00 and 9:00 a.m. on one weekday per quarter, routed on the transit schedules archived for that day. The value is the average over the usable quarters of the metro's latest year with usable schedules, which is 2025 for most metros (see Coverage).

Car

Driving times on the street network with no traffic. The car counts are an upper bound, since real trips at rush hour are slower.

Jobs

All jobs in the Census Bureau's LODES, counted at their workplaces, in 2023, the latest year available when the data were built. The transit values for 2024 and 2025 also count 2023's jobs, so both modes count the same jobs. LODES has no data after 2016 for Alaska or after 2021 for Michigan, so the metros in those states count jobs from those years.

Colors

Each color holds about a tenth of the block groups. Red is below the median block group and teal is above it. With "This view", the tenths are recomputed for the block groups on screen. On the car vs. transit map the colors run the other way, so red marks the largest car advantage.
